Next Steps

What to book, when to book it, and in what order.

BOOKING CHECKLIST

  • 1
    Choose your option — A or B Everything else depends on this decision. Once you pick, the rest flows naturally.
  • 2
    Book flights immediately Nonstop routes on this corridor are limited and fill fast. Target now for best pricing. Option A: TPA→MSP + ORD→TPA. Option B: TPA→SLC + BZN→PDX + SEA→TPA.
  • 3
    Book boutique hotels immediately Washington School House (12 rooms — sells out months ahead), Hotel Jackson, Hewing Hotel, Lora Hotel, The Robey. These small boutique properties have very limited inventory for summer dates.
  • 4
    Reserve Owamni by The Sioux Chef (Option A) This is the #1 reservation to secure. James Beard winner, small dining room, books weeks out. Reservations open on Resy — set an alert.
  • 5
    Reserve Kann (Option B — Portland) Top Chef winner Gregory Gourdet’s restaurant. Dedicated GF+DF. One of the most sought-after reservations in Portland. Book as early as possible.
  • 6
    Book rental car(s) Option A: One-way MSP→ORD, Enterprise or National (for status points). Book now for best rate. Option B: SLC→BZN + PDX→SEA. Book both at once.
  • 7
    Reserve Colita & Architecture Cruise (Option A) Colita in Minneapolis: small room, popular. Chicago Architecture Center River Cruise: popular on summer weekends — book a specific time slot.
  • 8
    Option B: Book Grand Teton activities Jenny Lake boat shuttle (first-come-first-served, arrive early). Snake River float trip in Jackson — book with a guide company (Barker-Ewing, etc.).
  • 9
    Remaining hotel nights & dining The Edgewater Madison, Dörr/Hillside Door County, Hotel Lucia/Woodlark Portland, Thompson/State Hotel Seattle. Then remaining restaurant reservations in each city.
  • 10
    Pack GF road snacks for mountain days (Option B) Order or pack GF crackers, bars, trail mix. The Good Food GF Bakery stop in SLC on Day 1 is your supply run for Days 3–6 in mountain country.

Important Notes
Always mention celiac disease (not just "gluten-free preference") when booking restaurants. This triggers stricter kitchen protocols.
Washington School House has only 12 rooms and is the hardest hotel to book — prioritize this one above all others for Option B.
Owamni is the most important restaurant reservation on the entire trip (Option A). Use Resy and set up alerts for your dates.
One-way car rental fees: Enterprise/National typically charge $100–200 for one-way drops. Factor into budget comparison.
June weather window: June 11–20 is generally the sweet spot — post-Memorial Day crowds, pre-July 4th crowds, best weather in both regions.
